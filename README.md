StartupForge AI 🚀

StartupForge AI is an AI-powered startup operating system designed to help entrepreneurs, students, and solo founders transform a raw business idea into a structured, launch-ready startup.

Instead of using multiple tools for market research, business planning, branding, financial planning, marketing, and investor preparation, StartupForge AI brings these workflows together in one intelligent platform.

🌟 What is StartupForge AI?

Starting a company is difficult because founders have to handle many different tasks:

Finding and validating an idea
Understanding the target market
Researching competitors
Defining customers
Creating a business model
Planning pricing and revenue
Building a brand
Creating marketing strategies
Preparing investor materials
Planning product development
Tracking startup growth
